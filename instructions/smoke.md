# Building Your Own Proton Pack Smoke Machine

Want to add a smoke effect to your Ghostbusters Proton Pack? This guide covers the tools, materials, and smoke fluid for a compact smoke machine using a 5V air pump and a 1.8-ohm vaporizer. The diagram below shows the layout of my build.

## 1. Tools You'll Need

- Soldering iron
- Dremel with a grinding bit
- Scissors or utility knife
- Hot glue gun
- Wire stripper

## 2. Materials

- Solder
- High-temperature industrial hot glue
- Electrical tape
- **Heat-shrink tubing**
- Red and black electrical wires
- 6 mm PVC air hose
- 6 mm quick-connect fitting (optional, but convenient)
- 5V air pump
- 5V PC cooling fan
- 1.8-ohm vaporizer (Kanger T2)
- TalentCell power bank with a 12V output
- Suitable buck converter for the 5V pump and fan
- A separately regulated, appropriately rated power supply for the vaporizer
- Suitable connectors, switching and electrical protection

The quick-connect fitting makes it easier to detach the hose for servicing and refilling. Check whether your hose is specified by its inner or outer diameter before buying a fitting.

The diagram illustrates the battery, buck converter, relay, air pump, fan, vaporizer and 6 mm hose routing. It is a layout reference, **not a verified electrical design**. Before wiring anything, confirm the exact model and ratings of your battery, pump, fan, converter, relay and vaporizer.

**Important electrical and heat precautions:** The 1.8-ohm vaporizer must not be connected directly to the TalentCell 12V output. The diagram also appears to show it on a shared 5V line, but its permitted coil voltage must be checked and supplied from a suitable, separately regulated output rather than assumed to be 5V. Keep hot glue away from the coil and other heated surfaces. Use heat-shrink tubing to insulate soldered electrical joints, and use a suitable driver and protection for any higher-current load.

## 4. Smoke Fluid

The intended smoke fluid for this build consists of:

| Ingredient | Proportion | Purity |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Propylene glycol (PG) | 40% | 99.5% |
| Plant-based vegetable glycerin (VG) | 60% | 99.5% |

**No water is included in this recipe.** Specify whether the percentages are by weight or volume if mixing your own fluid; these are not interchangeable. This recipe is not a safety certification for inhalation or for this modified vaporizer. Heated PG/VG can produce harmful breakdown products, especially if a coil overheats or runs dry. Avoid inhaling the aerosol directly, use sparingly in a well-ventilated area, and keep it away from other people and animals.

## 5. Before You Start Building

Check all component voltage and current ratings. Set and verify any adjustable converter's output before connecting its load. Keep the vaporizer upright and away from heat-sensitive parts; ensure the air outlet is not blocked and leaking fluid cannot reach electrical connections. The air hose must connect to the intended airflow path, not pressurize the liquid reservoir. Never operate the coil dry or unattended.
